The TypeScript core is the product the plugins sit on. Its design is settled: 41 Spec Kit
features, 1,643 functional requirements, and a parity suite that the port has to pass against
today's CLI before any release. It is being written by Specstride itself; see
[Built by itself](/#built-by-itself).
